usage: {prog} move [-h] [--debug] NUM|EMAIL|ALIAS SLOT

Assign an account to a slot number. An empty slot relocates the account there and frees its old slot; an occupied slot swaps the two. Aliases, backups, and session history move with the account.

positional arguments:
  NUM|EMAIL|ALIAS  Account to move
  SLOT             Destination slot number

options:
  -h, --help       show this help message and exit
  --debug          Enable debug logging

Examples:
  {prog} move user@example.com 1   move an account onto shortcut 1
  {prog} move dev 1                by alias
  {prog} move 2 1                  by number (swaps if slot 1 is taken)

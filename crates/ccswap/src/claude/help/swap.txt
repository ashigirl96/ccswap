usage: {prog} claude swap [-h] [--debug] NUM|EMAIL|ALIAS NUM|EMAIL|ALIAS

Exchange two accounts' slot numbers, so they trade places in `{prog} list` and as numeric targets. Aliases, backups, and session history move with their account.

positional arguments:
  NUM|EMAIL|ALIAS  One account
  NUM|EMAIL|ALIAS  The other account

options:
  -h, --help       show this help message and exit
  --debug          Enable debug logging

Examples:
  {prog} swap 1 2
  {prog} swap dev user@example.com
